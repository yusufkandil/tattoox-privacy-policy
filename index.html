TattooX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

TattooX ("the App") allows users to preview tattoo designs and explore tattoo styles. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the App.

1. Information and Permissions

The App may request access to your camera and photo library so you can take or select photos and preview tattoo designs. Photos and tattoo images you select are used to provide the App's features.

The App may also process technical information, device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostics, and usage information through third-party services.

2. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data, subject to their own policies and applicable settings.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy.

3. In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases. Payments are handled through the applicable app store's billing system. TattooX does not ask users to provide their full payment card details directly through the App.

4. How Information Is Used

  • Provide and maintain App features.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Process purchases.
  • Diagnose errors and improve performance.
  • Help protect the App against misuse.

5. Data Sharing and Retention

Third-party service providers, including advertising and app-store providers, may process information as needed to provide their services.

Photos selected by users are used for tattoo preview features. The App's actual data handling depends on how each feature is implemented. Information may be retained for as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by applicable law. Third-party providers may have their own retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

TattooX is not intended for children under 13 and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a revised update date.
